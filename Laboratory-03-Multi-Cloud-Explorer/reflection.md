# Mission Reflection

This cloud computing mission helped me understand the differences between **AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform (GCP)**. The platform that impressed me the most was **Google Cloud** because of its strong AI, machine learning, data analytics, and Kubernetes services.

I learned that AWS, Azure, and GCP all provide important services such as **virtual machines, storage, databases, networking, and security**. However, each platform has different strengths. **AWS** offers many services, **Azure** works well with Microsoft products, and **GCP** is strong in AI, data analytics, and Kubernetes.

I also learned that choosing a cloud platform depends on the **needs of the business**. Factors such as budget, existing technology, security, performance, and future growth should be considered. For example, a Microsoft-based company may prefer Azure, while an AI-focused company may choose GCP.

As a Cloud Solutions Consultant, I learned that there is no single best cloud platform for everyone. The best choice depends on the **client's goals and requirements**. I also learned how to compare services and recommend suitable cloud solutions.

Finally, this mission helped me improve my **GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio**. I was able to organize my research, comparisons, recommendations, and Linux investigation. Overall, the mission gave me a better understanding of cloud computing and made me more confident in explaining and comparing cloud platforms.
